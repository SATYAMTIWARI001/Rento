🏠 RENTO — Rent. Share. Earn.

A peer-to-peer marketplace for renting products and accessing local services.

RENTO is a modern rental marketplace designed to make renting simple, affordable, and trustworthy. It connects people who have products or services to offer with people who need them — without requiring everyone to buy everything they use.

From electronics and furniture to vehicles, tools, equipment, and local services, RENTO brings different rental needs together in one platform.

⸻

🚀 The Idea

Buying something you only need for a few days can be expensive and wasteful.

At the same time, many people have unused products, skills, or services that could generate additional income.

RENTO connects both sides.

👤 For Customers

Find what you need, compare options, check availability, and rent it for the required period.

🏪 For Sellers / Providers

List products or services, set your pricing and availability, and earn from assets or services you already have.

⸻

🎯 Problem

Traditional ownership creates several problems:

* Expensive products are often used only occasionally.
* People purchase items they don’t need long-term.
* Unused products remain idle.
* Finding trustworthy local rental options can be difficult.
* Small service providers often lack a digital marketplace.
* Existing rental platforms can be focused on only one category.

RENTO’s Approach

Instead of building separate platforms for every rental category, RENTO creates one flexible marketplace for products and services.
